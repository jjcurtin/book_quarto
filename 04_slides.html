4  Slide Decks

4.1 Your report is almost a deck

In the terminal, copy your report:

cp report.qmd slides.qmd

In slides.qmd, replace the format: lines with one line:

---
title: "Ice Cover on Lake Mendota"
author: "Your Name"
date: today
format: revealjs
---
  • revealjs is Quarto’s format for slides in a web browser
  • Yes, this copies your code; the paper unit comes back to that

Render, then open slides.html in your browser:

quarto render slides.qmd
  • Use the arrow keys to move between slides

4.2 What you got

You changed one line, and:

  • The YAML header made a title slide
  • Each ## heading started a new slide
  • The code is gone: decks hide code by default

But one slide has a problem:

  • The figure shares its slide with your three sentences
  • Quarto shrinks the figure to make room for them

4.3 One idea per slide

Add a heading just above your sentences:

## The numbers

Before 1900, the lake averaged `r mean_1800s` days of ice.
Since 2000, it has averaged `r mean_recent`.
  • The sentences move to a new slide
  • The figure now has its slide to itself, and it grows to fill it

Want a new slide without a title?

  • Put --- on a line by itself, with blank lines around it

4.4 Bullets and pauses

Slides read better as short bullets than as sentences:

## The numbers

- Before 1900: `r mean_1800s` days of ice per winter
- Since 2000: `r mean_recent` days

. . .

- Shortest winter: `r min(ice$ice_days)` days
- Longest winter: `r max(ice$ice_days)` days

. . . on a line by itself is a pause.

  • The slide shows everything above it first
  • Press → to show the rest
  • Keep blank lines around it

4.5 Speaker notes

At the bottom of a slide, add:

::: {.notes}
About a month less ice than in the 1800s.
:::
  • Everything between ::: {.notes} and ::: is a note
  • Your audience doesn’t see notes

Press S for speaker view:

  • Your notes, a timer, and the next slide
  • If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the page

4.6 Your turn

ImportantTry it
  1. Add the ## The numbers heading, render, and check that the figure has its own slide
  2. Turn your sentences into bullets, with a . . . pause
  3. Add a speaker note to one slide, render, and press S

4.7 Optional: publish your deck

First, give your deck its own title in the YAML header:

title: "Less Ice on Lake Mendota"
  • Connect Cloud takes the title from your YAML, but only on the first publish
  • Your report already uses “Ice Cover on Lake Mendota”

Then publish, just like the report:

quarto publish posit-connect-cloud slides.qmd
  • The deck gets its own URL
  • _publish.yml adds a second entry, for slides.qmd

4.8 Where we are

We have:

  • Turned the report into a slide deck by changing format:
  • Split slides with ## headings, so each idea gets its own slide
  • Added bullets, pauses, and speaker notes
